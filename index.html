Equipment Alert Notifications

Business / Service: Xiao's Automation Alerts
Contact: 512201627@qq.com

Use case (summary)

We send automated, transactional SMS alerts to authorized customers when their industrial equipment reports abnormal conditions (high temperature, pressure, system faults). These messages are strictly operational — no marketing or promotional content is sent.

How opt-in is collected

Customers explicitly opt in to receive SMS alerts during device onboarding or by signing a service agreement. Example opt-in flows include:

  • Customer completes a device registration form (checkbox: "I agree to receive equipment alert SMS").
  • Customer sends an SMS keyword to enroll or confirms via email/console during setup.
  • Customer signs up via a contract / onboarding form and provides phone number and consent.

Opt-in type: Via Text / During Onboarding

Customers may opt out anytime by contacting support at 512201627@qq.com.

Sample messages

[Alert] Your equipment reported a high temperature warning at 18:32. Please check the system immediately.
[System Notification] Your device is back to normal. Status: OK.
